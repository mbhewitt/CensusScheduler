Helping a Stranger

In the 2018 and 2019 online surveys participants were also asked the question, "Please think about what you would do in the following situation. Suppose you were given 14 hours of free time off from work, and had the opportunity to spend any amount of this time from 0-14 hours, doing a personal favor for a random stranger. Outside of this favour, you would never see this person again, and the stranger would not know how much time you were given in total. If you were given 14 hours of free time, how much (from 0-14) would you spend doing a favor for the stranger?"
